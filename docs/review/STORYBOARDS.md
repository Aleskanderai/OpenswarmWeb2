# OpenSwarm — 30 direction storyboards

186 scenes, with six or seven scenes in every direction. 24 openings lead with image, typography, ASCII, a diagram or an image index; 6 lead with a product surface.

[Open the interactive review](http://localhost:5173/) · [Open the static review](index.html)

These are interactive website storyboards. Each scene describes a page moment, its motion intent and an action to review. They are not thirty production sites.

The working examples use local sample state. The Evidence Lab chapters show the supplied original app captures with their disclosures intact. Motion includes three existing scenic films, a supplied product recording, live character fields and CSS/SVG transitions; scene notes also describe the intended continuity for a finished scroll experience.
